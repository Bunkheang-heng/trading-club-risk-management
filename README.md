# Risk Management & Capital Preservation — AUPP Trading Club

An ultra-clean, visual, and mathematical presentation slide deck teaching **Risk Management, Capital Preservation, Position Sizing, and Mathematical Edge** for mentees in the **AUPP Trading Club** (American University of Phnom Penh).

## Overview
Designed to be institutional, punchy, and beginner-friendly with minimal text and maximum visual clarity:
- **The 3 Pillars of Trading Diagram**: A clean, simplified connected triad connecting Strategy, **Risk Management (TODAY'S TOPIC)**, and Psychology into a central "Trader" hub.
- **Capital Is Inventory**: Defense precedes offense. Preserving capital ensures your trading business stays open.
- **The 1% - 2% Rule**: Fixed fractional risk model that lets you comfortably survive an 8-trade losing streak.
- **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR)**: Understanding the Win-Rate Paradox (why 40% accuracy produces strong positive profit at 1:3 RRR).
- **Position Sizing Formula**: The exact mathematical equation to compute precise lot sizes based on stop loss distance.
- **Drawdown Mathematics**: The asymmetric recovery trap (why losing 50% requires a 100% gain to break even).
- **Live Interactive Position Size Calculator**: Built right into the slide deck for live classroom demonstrations.
- **4 Golden Rules**: Never widen stop loss, 2-loss daily limit, pre-defined risk, and leverage reality.
